How our software products will work

One product, one app, one set of accounts.

Each Atherbyte SaaS product will be its own independent application. This website is the place to discover them, and each product will link out to its own app when it launches.

  • Its own application

    Every product runs on its own, separate from this website and from the other products.

  • Its own accounts

    Accounts are independent for each product. Signing up for one product does not create an account in another.

  • Its own plans

    Any subscription plans and billing will belong to the individual product, and will be announced when that product launches.

  • Its own data

    Each product keeps its own data, built around what that product needs.

Nothing on this page can be bought or subscribed to today.
