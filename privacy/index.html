Privacy Policy

This policy explains what information Atherbyte Studio handles through this website, the Atherbyte account service and the project form, and why. It describes how the service is built today.

1. Who we are

Atherbyte Studio is an independent digital products studio. This policy covers atherbyte.online (this website), the Atherbyte account service used at /account/, and the project inquiry form.

It does not cover separate products with their own sign-in and privacy practices. In particular, Atherbyte Queue (queue.atherbyte.online) is a separate application and handles its own data. Read the privacy information inside that product.

Privacy contact: atherbyte.studio@gmail.com.

2. Information we handle

Visitors

You can browse this website without an account. The pages are served by GitHub Pages, which, like any web host, receives technical information such as your IP address and browser details when your browser requests a page. We do not run our own analytics on these pages.

Most pages load Google's AdSense script. This website does not place any ad units of its own. Google may use cookies or similar technologies and process data to provide and measure ads, under Google's own policies. The account pages (/account/) do not load it.

Accounts

  • Username and password. Passwords are passed to our authentication provider (Supabase Auth) and we do not keep a copy ourselves.
  • A placeholder internal address that is created for every account. It cannot receive mail and is never shown to anyone.
  • Optional recovery email. If you add one, it is stored in the authentication provider and used only to send the verification link and password-reset links.
  • Optional profile picture, available after your email is verified (PNG, JPEG or WebP, up to 200 KB). Pictures are stored in a public storage bucket, which means anyone who has the picture's address can view it. You can remove it from your account page.
  • Technical data used to protect sign-in: Cloudflare Turnstile checks your browser when you register, sign in or request a reset, and your IP address is used for rate limits and sent to Turnstile for that check.
  • If you link a Grid Snake player to your account: the link between the player and your account, the player's name and best score (which can appear on the Grid Snake leaderboard), and the saved progress, including up to 20 earlier saved copies. Each link attempt is also recorded in a log (account, player, outcome and time; no recovery codes or names).

Project form

If you use the project inquiry form we receive what you type: your name, email, project type and description, and, if you choose to give them, a budget range, a reference link and a preferred contact method. The same security check and rate limits apply. You can also email us directly instead.

3. Why we use it, and on what basis

  • To create and run your account, keep you signed in and let you recover access (providing the service you asked for).
  • To answer your project inquiry or email (taking steps at your request, and our legitimate interest in running a studio).
  • To keep the service safe: bot checks, rate limits and abuse prevention (our legitimate interest).
  • To show and measure ads through Google AdSense, where it is active (Google's processing under its own terms).

Where data-protection law such as the GDPR applies to you, these are the legal bases we rely on.

4. Where it is stored, and who else is involved

We use service providers who process information on our behalf or to deliver the service:

  • GitHub Pages: hosts the website.
  • Cloudflare: runs our API (Cloudflare Workers), the Turnstile check and rate limiting.
  • Supabase: stores account records, recovery email status, profile pictures, Grid Snake account links and saves, and project inquiries.
  • An email delivery provider configured in Supabase sends verification and reset emails. Google (Gmail) receives emails you send to us.
  • Google AdSense: advertising script on public pages, as described above.

These providers may process data in countries other than yours. We share information with a provider only as needed for the purposes above, or if the law requires it.

5. Cookies, sessions and local storage

Our own pages do not set cookies. When you sign in, your session (an access token and a refresh token) is kept in your browser's local storage, shared across atherbyte.online so the account and the games recognise you. Signing out revokes the session on the server and removes it from your browser. If you choose a language, that choice is also kept in local storage on your device.

Google AdSense, and Cloudflare Turnstile on the account page, may use cookies or similar technologies of their own.

Our Grid Snake and Stop the Timer games keep progress on your device. A game's own page explains what it stores.

6. How long we keep it

Account information is kept until you ask us to delete your account. Access tokens are short-lived, and your session is revoked when you sign out. You can remove your profile picture yourself. Project inquiries sent through the form are kept for 12 months and then deleted by us. Earlier saved copies of Grid Snake progress are capped at 20 per player. Account records are not deleted automatically.

There is no delete button on the account page. To have your account deleted, email atherbyte.studio@gmail.com from the address you used and tell us your username; the same applies to any other information you want deleted. We carry out deletion by hand, and removing Grid Snake player records is a separate manual step. We delete information we are able to delete, and we cannot guarantee that copies held by providers, backups or content you published elsewhere disappear immediately.

7. How we protect it

Measures that exist in the service today:

  • Pages and the API are served over HTTPS.
  • The API accepts browser requests only from an allow-list of origins and validates input on the server.
  • Sign-in, registration, password reset and the project form use a bot check, and the API applies rate limits.
  • Session tokens are sent in a request header, not in cookies, and the account page has a strict content-security policy.
  • Passwords are handled by the authentication provider. Resetting a password signs out the account's sessions.
  • Secret keys stay on the server and are not published in this website's code. The API does not log request bodies.
  • Profile pictures can be changed only through our server, which checks type, size and that your email is verified.

No system is completely secure, and we cannot promise absolute protection. Choose a strong, unique password and add a recovery email.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask to access, correct, delete or export your personal information, to object to or restrict some processing, and to complain to your local data-protection authority.

To make a request, email atherbyte.studio@gmail.com from the address you used, and tell us your username for account requests. We may need to confirm that the request is yours before acting on it.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change how we handle information, we will update this page and its date. Significant changes will be shown on this page.
