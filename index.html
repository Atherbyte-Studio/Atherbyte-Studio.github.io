About the studio

An independent studio, building for the browser.

Atherbyte Studio is an independent studio focused on games, software and creative digital products. We make things that run right in your browser, with nothing to download or install.

So far we've released two games, Grid Snake and Stop the Timer. Next, we're opening up to custom projects for people and businesses who want something built for them.
