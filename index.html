Product philosophy

Fewer things, finished properly.

We are a product studio first. That changes how we build for clients too.

  1. 01

    We own what we ship

    Our products run on our own accounts, our own servers and our own responsibility. We know what it takes to keep software working.

  2. 02

    Honest about status

    Live, available or in development: every product says which one it is. Nothing is presented as more finished than it is.

  3. 03

    Fast, light, accessible

    Pages that load quickly, work on phones and keyboards, and respect people who ask for reduced motion.

  4. 04

    Small teams, direct talk

    You deal with the people who build the work. We would rather say "not a fit" than promise everything.

How we work

A clear path from idea to launch.

Four steps, and you know where you are in each of them.

  1. 1

    Discover

    We listen, ask what the project must achieve and who it is for, and agree what is in and out of scope.

  2. 2

    Design

    Structure, flows and visuals. You see the direction before the heavy building starts.

  3. 3

    Build

    Iterative development with working versions you can open and comment on along the way.

  4. 4

    Launch & improve

    We release it, check it works in the real world, and keep improving what matters.

Technology & craft

Chosen for the job, not for fashion.

This is what our own products and this website are actually built with.

Web platform

This website is hand-built HTML, CSS and JavaScript, served from GitHub Pages. No framework, no tracking scripts of our own.

Cloudflare Workers

Our account API and contact handling run on Cloudflare Workers, with bot checks (Turnstile) and rate limits in front of them.

Supabase

Accounts and data for our own products are stored in Supabase (Postgres, Auth and Storage), reached only through our API.

Next.js & TypeScript

Atherbyte Queue is a Next.js and TypeScript application deployed on Cloudflare.
