About

An independent digital products company.

Atherbyte Studio is an independent digital products company focused on building games, SaaS products, software tools, and custom digital experiences.

What we do

We build and own our products.

We create browser games and software products ourselves, and we use that experience to build custom websites and software for businesses.

So far we have released two games, Grid Snake and Stop the Timer. Atherbyte Mind Clash and a dental clinic website project are in development.

  • Games

    Browser games that are ready to play the moment the page loads.

  • SaaS & software products

    Software products and tools, each planned as its own independent application.

  • Development services

    Websites, web applications and custom software for businesses.

How we approach it

Principles we work by.

  • Honest about status

    If something is unfinished, we say so. Every product shows its real state.

  • Quality over volume

    A small number of products, built with care, rather than a long list of half-finished ones.

  • Fast and accessible

    Lightweight pages that load quickly and work on phones, tablets and desktops.
